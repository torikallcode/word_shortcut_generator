# Taste

## Workflow

- Wants a written PRD/plan document (including scope breakdown, phases, and open questions) produced and reviewed before implementation of a new feature begins. Confidence: 0.75

## Product behavior

- Prefers fully automatic, single-trigger actions ("satu tekan tanpa dialog") over semi-automated flows that open a dialog or prompt — accepts heavier machinery (e.g. macros) rather than making the user click through steps. Confidence: 0.6
